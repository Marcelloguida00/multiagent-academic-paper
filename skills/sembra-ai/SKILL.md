# Sembra-AI: The Anti-AI Humanizer & De-Slop Engine

This skill inspects drafts and transforms them into prose that reads like high-caliber, peer-reviewed human academic scholarship.

## When to Use
- Whenever drafting or refining any section of an academic paper.
- Triggered automatically by the multi-agent pipeline prior to finalizing any section.
- Triggered manually when the user calls `/sembra-ai` on an existing text block.

---

## The 6-Step De-Slop & Humanization Audit

When text is passed into `sembra-ai`, evaluate and rewrite it against these six criteria:

### 1. Cliché Eradication (Banned Markers)
Strip the following typical LLM signatures:
- Fillers and throat-clearing openers (*"It is important to remember that"*, *"In the contemporary landscape"*, *"Needless to say"*, *"A testament to"*, *"A tapestry of"*).
- Generic positive adjectives (*"Pivotal"*, *"Crucial"*, *"Transformative"*, *"Revolutionary"*, *"Seamless"*, *"Game-changer"*). Replace with concrete functional descriptions.

### 2. Burstiness & Cadence Tuning
- LLM text tends to have uniform sentence lengths (~18-22 words per sentence, comma-separated mid-clause).
- **Human rhythm**: Mix very long compound sentences (synthesizing methodology, causal trade-offs, and conditions) with punchy 5-to-10-word declarative conclusions.

### 3. Structural Asymmetry
- LLMs love symmetrical trios: three adjectives, three bullet points, three subheadings per section.
- **Human structure**: Use irregular structures. If point 1 has two counter-examples, point 2 might be a brief qualification, and point 3 a deep empirical dive.

### 4. Scholarly Skepticism & Friction
- AI drafts sound overly confident, cheerful, and promotional.
- Human researchers highlight friction: architectural trade-offs, compute bottlenecks, communication latency, non-deterministic failures, edge-case degradation, and unverified assumptions.

### 5. Terminology Formalization (Terminology Sanitization)
- Ensure all colloquialisms or platform-specific tool names are converted into formal academic vocabulary (e.g., *Blackboard pattern*, *Consensus protocol*, *Hierarchical orchestration*, *Iterative convergence*).

### 6. Zero Self-Reference
- The text must never refer to itself as being generated by an AI, a skill, a prompt, or an agent assistant.

---

## Output Standard

When reviewing a draft:
1. Provide the cleaned, de-slopped final draft directly.
2. Provide a brief audit summary:
   - **Formulas removed**: Which clichés were eliminated.
   - **Rhythm adjustments**: How sentence flow was varied.
   - **Tone status**: Clean, scholarly, verified human-sounding.
