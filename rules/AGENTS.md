# Operational Rules for Academic Paper & Multi-Agent Agency

These rules are strictly enforced across all agents, writers, and reviewers working on research and paper writing.

## 1. The Anti-AI Filter Protocol (`/sembra-ai`)
- **Mandatory Quality Gate**: No section, paragraph, or draft may be considered final without passing through the humanization and de-slopping pipeline.
- **Banned LLM Hallmarks & Clichés**:
  - Prohibited formulaic openers: *"In today's fast-paced world"*, *"It is crucial to note that"*, *"Delving into"*, *"A tapestry of"*, *"Beacon of innovation"*, *"Testament to"*, *"Moreover, furthermore, in conclusion"* as repetitive transitions.
  - Symmetrical list fatigue: Do not default to neat 3-point bullet lists for everything. Real academic research features asymmetric, nuanced discussion and counter-arguments.
- **Burstiness and Sentence Length Variation**:
  - Alternate long analytical sentences (explaining causal mechanisms) with short, assertive declarative statements.
  - Maintain a critical, scholarly human voice (scholarly skepticism, acknowledgment of practical limitations, edge cases).

## 2. Strict Tool & Skill Isolation (Zero Leakage)
- **NEVER cite internal tools, skills, or prompt mechanics in the paper**:
  - Do NOT write: *"We used the skill search_web"*, *"The research agent invoked skill_xyz"*, or *"Our prompt engineering workflow"*.
  - INSTEAD write: *"The orchestration layer leverages a decentralized task allocation protocol"*, *"Information retrieval was conducted via automated querying of academic repositories (IEEE, ACM, ArXiv)"*, *"Coordination is governed by a blackboard architectural pattern"*.
- The paper must stand alone as pure scientific literature.

## 3. Rigorous Terminology Sanitization
- Replace colloquial, casual, or informal names with formal academic terms:
  - *"Bot / AI agent"* -> *Autonomous Computational Agent / Specialized Autonomous Unit*
  - *"Messaggini / bacheca"* -> *Asynchronous Shared-Memory Protocol / Blackboard Message Exchange*
  - *"Capo agente"* -> *Hierarchical Coordinator / Dispatcher*
  - *"Fare il riassuntone"* -> *Knowledge Synthesis & Abstractive Condensation*
- Maintain uniform terminology throughout the entire document.

## 4. Academic Sources & Bibliographical Integrity
- Every empirical, historical, or theoretical claim must cite real, verifiable peer-reviewed papers or seminal preprints (ArXiv, IEEE, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACM).
- Document full metadata for each entry: Authors, Title, Conference/Journal, Year, and DOI or ArXiv ID.
- Maintain formal typographic formatting adhering to IEEE, ACM, or Springer LaTeX standards (Computer Modern or Times New Roman).
